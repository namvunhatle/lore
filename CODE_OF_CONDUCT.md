# Code of Conduct

We want Lore's community to be welcoming, whatever your background, experience or language. Vietnamese and English are both fine in issues and pull requests.

## Our standard

Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people. Give feedback that helps someone improve, and accept it the same way. Credit others' work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about someone's language or accent.
- Discriminatory jokes or content.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwanted attention.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or attempts to use the project for malware, scams or surveillance.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block accounts that break these rules. To report a problem, use the private contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (GitHub's private reporting) and mention "conduct". Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, discussions and any event where someone represents the project.
