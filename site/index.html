One ordinary day.

Here is how Lore fits into it.

7:40 AM

You wonder something. You just ask.

On the train you type a question into the one bar, not an address. Lore answers in a line or two and shows where it came from. It never guesses a price or a flight.

9:15 AM

A long article, a short morning.

Lore sits beside the page. It sums it up, pulls out the dates, or answers "does it mention a price?" by quoting the exact line.

Sample summary from a stand-in model.

10:20 AM

Twelve tabs, one tidy-up.

Too many tabs open? Lore reads their titles and groups them by what they are about: a trip, a project, a topic. You see the plan first, change what you like, press Apply, and undo if you change your mind. Duplicates are found too.

Groups made by a stand-in model.

11:30 AM

The email you've been putting off.

Tell Lore what you need. It writes the draft and opens it in your own Gmail or Outlook. You read it, you press Send. That last part stays yours.

Sample draft from a stand-in model.

1:00 PM

Read on one side, write on the other.

Open two pages side by side and drag the divider where you like. Click into either one and Lore follows, so the tools and the address always belong to the page you are on.

2:00 PM

Wait, where was that page?

You remember the idea, not the address. Ask by meaning and Lore finds it, from a memory that is encrypted and lives only on your computer.

Build from source

Needs Node.js 22+ and Git.

git clone https://github.com/tevo-web/lore.git
cd lore/desktop
npm ci
npm run filters   # compile the ad-block lists (needs internet)
npm start

Questions

Is it free?

Yes: open source, no account, no ads. A cloud AI key you add may cost money. A model on your computer does not.

Is it really private?

No analytics, no telemetry. Your history and memory stay on your computer, encrypted. "Private" is not "anonymous": sites still see your connection, and a cloud AI sees what you send it. Every result says where text went.

Do I need an AI model?

No. Answers, memory, ad blocking and scam warnings work without one. A model adds writing, summaries and translation.

Can I bring my bookmarks and history?

Yes, from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Cốc Cốc and Firefox on this computer, or from a bookmarks file. Passwords are not imported.

Does Lore update itself?

It checks for new versions, and on Windows and Linux installs them for you. On macOS it tells you and opens the download page until the app is signed.

Can I sign in to Google?

Sometimes. Google blocks some embedded browsers. If it says no, Lore offers to open the page in your system browser.

Android or iPhone?

Planned, not started.
